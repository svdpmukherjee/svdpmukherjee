# Hi, I am Suvadeep (a.k.a Suvo)

**Experimental researcher who builds the full stack.** I work at the fusion of UX, behavioural science, full-stack development and applied AI, focused on the **human side of cybersecurity**: usable security and social engineering.

I hold a **PhD in Psychology (Human-Computer Interaction)**, and I do something that usually takes a team. I start from a high-stakes, real-world problem: I study its impact, dig into how people actually perceive and experience it, and let that understanding shape the work. Only then do I form a hypothesis, design the experiment to test it, build the full-stack platform that runs it, integrate the AI that powers it, and analyse the behavioural data that comes back. Because the same person owns the problem framing, the psychology, the UX and the code, none of them get lost in translation.

Currently a postdoctoral researcher at the **Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT), University of Luxembourg**, working on phishing-susceptibility research: why people fall for phishing at work, how to model that decision formally, and whether AI personas can stand in for real users when testing defenses.

---

### What I do, end to end

- **Experimental psychology** · design controlled, ecologically valid studies (factorial designs, individual-difference measures) that isolate _why_ people behave the way they do.
- **UX & user-centred design** · prototype, pilot with target users, measure, iterate, then turn findings into concrete design decisions (Figma, usability testing, thematic analysis).
- **Full-stack engineering** · build the realistic web platforms that run those studies and silently capture rich behavioural data (actions, timing, clicks, hovers) alongside surveys.
- **AI integration** · wire frontier and open-source LLMs into research and product pipelines to generate, evaluate and calibrate content, and to simulate human decisions.
- **Behavioural analysis** · close the loop, turning interaction traces into formal models and measurable results.

### Recent projects (pinned)

**[CYPEARL](https://github.com/svdpmukherjee/CYPEARL)** — Cyber Synthetic Persona Creation and Evaluation for Adversarial Resilience.
A connected research pipeline on workplace phishing susceptibility: a screening study grounds an ecologically valid email set, a simulated-inbox experiment captures how people judge phishing vs. legitimate mail, and an admin layer calibrates LLM-based personas against that human data, toward a safer, lower-cost way to test phishing defenses without exposing real employees. Built with React, Vite, Tailwind, FastAPI and MongoDB; containerised with Docker and initially deployed on AWS (now discontinued; only deployed in vercel/render). [Live demo →](https://cypearl.vercel.app/)

**[game-behavior-intervention-study](https://github.com/svdpmukherjee/game-behavior-intervention-study)** — Behavioural-change interventions in remote assessment.
A reward-based anagram game that tracks player behaviour in detail, paired with an LLM-driven framework that generates and optimises motivational messages (grounded in behavioural theories such as Self-Determination, Cognitive Dissonance, Self-Efficacy and Social Norm theory) to study honest play and cheating. Full-stack React / FastAPI / MongoDB, with a human-in-the-loop message pipeline and a behavioural-analytics stage. [Live demo →](https://anagram-solving-game-study.vercel.app/)

### How I work

I start by understanding the problem properly before building anything: reviewing the literature, scoping it with expert interviews, and running early user studies and interviews with the people who actually live it. From there I prototype, test with real users, measure, and iterate before committing to a direction. I care about clean flows, a consistent visual language and code quality, and I use AI coding assistants (with structured prompting) as part of my day-to-day pipeline for faster, well-documented iteration. I am comfortable translating between technical and non-technical stakeholders, and I pick up new stacks quickly when a project calls for it.
