# Hi, I am Suvadeep Mukherjee (a.k.a Suvo)

I design, build and validate digital products end to end: working out what's worth building, shipping the full-stack app, and measuring whether it actually helps. I combine **user research, applied AI and hands-on engineering**.

Background: PhD in Human-Computer Interaction, and a recently completed postdoc at **SnT, University of Luxembourg**, researching the human side of cybersecurity (phishing and usable security). Now looking for **technical product development roles**.

🔗 **Portfolio of Suvadeep Mukherjee → [svdpmukherjee.github.io](https://svdpmukherjee.github.io)** — projects, experience and the full story.


### What I do, end to end

- **Experimentation & research design** · design controlled, real-world studies (factorial designs, A/B tests, individual-difference measures) that isolate _why_ people behave the way they do.
- **UX & user-centred design** · prototype, pilot with target users, measure, iterate, then turn findings into concrete design decisions (Figma, usability testing, thematic analysis).
- **Full-stack engineering** · build the realistic web platforms that run those studies and silently capture rich behavioural data (actions, timing, clicks, hovers) alongside surveys.
- **AI integration** · wire frontier and open-source LLMs into research and product pipelines to generate, evaluate and calibrate content, and to simulate human decisions.
- **Behavioural analysis** · close the loop, turning interaction traces into formal models and measurable results.
